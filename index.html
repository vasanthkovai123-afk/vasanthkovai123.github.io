What I believe

Three things most organisations get wrong about enterprise AI

01
The platform is the bottleneck, not the AI model.
Organisations spend months evaluating which LLM to use. The actual constraint is the quality, structure, and accessibility of the enterprise data underneath it. Fix the platform first.
02
AI in ERP delivery is already here — most programs just aren't using it.
Code quality gates, test scope recommendation, data migration intelligence — these aren't future capabilities. They can be built and deployed in weeks. The gap is awareness, not technology.
03
The next generation of CIOs will have shipped AI, not just sponsored it.
The executive credential that will matter in five years isn't "I oversaw an AI strategy." It's "I built AI into how my organisation works and measured the outcome." That requires being a practitioner, not a sponsor.
